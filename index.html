Bio

A media career spent in the spotlight, and helping others step into it.

Sandro Monetti is a leading journalist, broadcaster and media strategist frequently seen on CNN and the BBC. One of the most well connected and respected figures in media, he has written several books and serves on several boards, including the LA Press Club.

Alongside his public-facing work, Sandro is dedicated to mentorship, helping talented people reach their potential. His humanitarian work has been recognised by the White House and United Nations.

“My life’s work is to celebrate exceptional talent and tell the stories that allow it to be fully appreciated.”
